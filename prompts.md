# Structured Prompts

## Prompt 1: Homepage Copy Generation

Act as a professional website copywriter.

Create website copy for a salon called Glow & Style Salon located in Bangalore.

Target audience:
Women, working professionals, and college students.

Tone:
Friendly, modern, trustworthy.

Generate:
1. Homepage headline
2. Homepage subheadline
3. About section
4. Services section
5. Why choose us section
6. Contact CTA section

Make the content website-ready and conversion-focused.

---

## Prompt 2: Services Page Content

Create detailed service descriptions for:

1. Haircut & Styling
2. Hair Spa
3. Facial Treatments
4. Bridal Makeup
5. Manicure & Pedicure

For Glow & Style Salon, Bangalore.

Use a friendly and professional tone.

For each service include:
- Description
- What's Included
- Why Choose Us

---

## Prompt 3: CTA Section Generation

Generate 5 strong call-to-action sections for a salon website.

The CTAs should encourage:
- Appointment booking
- Calling the salon
- Visiting the salon
- Bridal consultations
- First-time customer offers

Business Name: Glow & Style Salon
Location: Bangalore

Use friendly and persuasive language.
